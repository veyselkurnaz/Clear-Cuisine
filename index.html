Frequently Asked Questions

Is Clear Cuisine free?

You can save up to 10 recipes for free. Upgrade to Plus for unlimited access.

Does it work without internet?

Yes! Once a recipe is extracted, it is stored locally on your device for offline access.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Extraction happens on your device using Apple's local AI models. No external servers.
